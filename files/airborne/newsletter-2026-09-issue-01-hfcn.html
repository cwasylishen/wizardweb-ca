New at Airborne Arts for 2027

Hi everyone. This is our first newsletter for 2027, and it is about your week: what is new at Airborne for 2027, and twelve retreats to choose from. Read it, then hit reply and tell me what you think.

New at Airborne Arts for 2027. More fun on arrival in San Jose pre retreat. More adventure on the journey down to Airborne Arts. More private spaces to choose for your room. More ways to get the stuff you want to make your time at Airborne amazing. More photo opportunities with our new Sky Swing, waterfall, and vanity photo shoots. More activities with our new dance poles. More learning with our new lecture sessions. More sun, water, beauty, fun, challenges, and connections await at Airborne Arts for the 2027 season.

 
 

The Sky Swing

Your apparatus, hung from a 100 foot tree, with a lush green valley, the Pacific Ocean and the sunset behind you. Epic photos, and an amazing swing out over the valley into the big blue.

See the Sky Swing
 
 

A private waterfall, just for you and your crew

On the same day as the Sky Swing, we can add a photo shoot at a lovely, gentle waterfall that is private and intimate. Shoot with lights, candles, or sparklers for a truly forest fairy vibe.

Photos you will be proud of

Every shoot can be photographed by you and your retreat mates. Or hire our professional photographer, who takes the shots, edits them, and delivers a gallery you will be proud of. Classes, private vanity sessions, group sessions, and night shots with our high end lighting and sound to get you in the mood to shine. Professional shoots cost extra and you can add them when you register.

Photo shoots and adventures
 
 

Eight dance poles

Six training poles and two practice poles, on our 20 by 40 foot dance and training deck and at the pool rancho. The two practice poles mean you can work on your own outside class instead of waiting for a turn.

Lecture sessions: how to thrive as a performer

We are working closely with our coaches to bring you skill building in your discipline and what you need to survive and thrive in the world of performance:

  • How to train
  • How to build your act
  • How to create a support system that ensures your success
  • Stories of what the pros have seen and learned on their journeys to greatness
 

The journey is now part of the retreat

We are working with hotels in San Jose to improve your pre retreat night: better food, and options for a bar and a hot tub. On the drive down we are adding a vista stop and an animal hike on the way to the beach, where we meet for a picnic before heading up to Airborne Arts. Your week starts before you get here.

Your room, your choice

Private rooms, from a glamping bell tent to the Master Suite, are on the website now. Pick yours when you register.

See the rooms
 
 

Tell us which

Love it or not?

Four ideas for 2027. I want your gut reaction on each one. One line is plenty.

  1. An end of week show by you and your coaches for the local community. I am working on bringing down my touring sound and lights to take those shows to a whole new level.
  2. A first night in San Jose worth showing up for. Hot tub party? Karaoke? Board games? Tell me what would be fun.
  3. A nature and beach view hike on the journey down, before you meet us for a picnic on the beach and a swim in our ocean.
  4. New photo opportunities with our pro photographer at the Sky Swing and the waterfall.
Reply with your picks
