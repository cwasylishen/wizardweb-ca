Private proposal, prepared for uvita.info
A proposal from Clinton, for Uvita Information Center

Your site already does its job.
Here's what would make it do more.

I went through uvita.info top to bottom this week the same way I'd check any client's site. The good news first: nothing is broken. Every link I tested works, the content is current, and your WhatsApp button is set up right where people expect it, on every page. That part doesn't need fixing. What I found is about weight and reach, not trust, and both are fixable without changing how the site looks or feels to you.

What's costing you visitors

The site is heavy,
and two contact paths
don't go anywhere.

Your homepage loads about 5.6 megabytes of photos before someone can even read it, close to 190 separate files, and takes roughly 6.6 seconds to finish on a normal connection. A big part of that is one logo file that's 970KB, loading on every single page. On hotel wifi or a phone with weak signal in the jungle, that's the difference between someone waiting for your page and someone leaving for the next Google result.

I also looked closer at how people actually reach you. The chat bubble that pops up on your site isn't WhatsApp, it's a separate chat tool, so a visitor who clicks it never lands in WhatsApp at all, they land somewhere you may not even be checking. And of the two phone numbers listed on your site, only +506 8843 7142 is a mobile that can open WhatsApp. The other, +506 2743 8889, is a landline. A visitor who taps that one hoping to message you reaches nothing.

    This wouldn't be a redesign. It would be the same tours, the same car rental and shuttle info, the same mail forwarding service, rebuilt underneath so it loads fast, reaches you directly, and shows up when people search before their trip.

    • 01Pages that load in about a second, not six or seven.Photos sized and compressed automatically, so they stay beautiful without costing you visitors on the way in.
    • 02A real English site and a real Spanish site.Two separate, properly indexed versions, not a translate widget bolted on top. Right now there's no Spanish version at all, which means every Spanish-speaking searcher, including people who already live around Uvita, never sees you.
    • 03A page for every tour and every service.Instead of one page listing everything at once, each tour and each service gets its own page that can actually rank for the exact thing someone typed, like a specific tour name or "Uvita snorkeling."
    • 04WhatsApp that actually opens WhatsApp.A WhatsApp button on every page, wired to your mobile number, opening the app directly. The dead landline link comes off, and the separate chat pop-up goes away, so there's exactly one way to reach you and it always works.
    • 05A simple dashboard, run by you.Change a tour price, swap a photo, or add a new listing yourself, without calling anyone or touching code.
