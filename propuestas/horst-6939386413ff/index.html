What we run

Two shops, one address.

When a job needs both trades, it does not leave the site to get them: a weldment gets fabricated, then machined to size. A worn component gets built up, re-cut and put back in the line.

Certified by the Canadian Welding Bureau

Horst Precision Machine is certified to CSA Standard W47.1, the certification of companies for fusion welding of steel. Where a drawing or a purchase order calls for that certification, the shop already qualifies.

Welding done in house, in the fabrication shop on Iron Mask Road.

Sectors we serve

Mines, mills, sawmills and structural steel.

Four sectors we have worked in extensively. Equipment costs money while it sits, so the part has to come back finished and right the first time.

Mining

Machining and large fabrication for mine and mill sites. New parts, repairs and rebuilds on components that come off site equipment.

Pulp & paper

Fabrication and machined parts for mill equipment.

Forestry & sawmilling

Parts and fabrication for sawmill and forestry operations, welded and machined on one site.

Structural steel

Cutting, forming and CWB-certified welding of structural steel, plus the machining that finishes it.
